🧠 An Extreme Marvel Trivia Challenge

Test your Marvel knowledge with 10 challenging questions inspired by the multiverse, Doctor Doom, the Fantastic Four, the X-Men, and the upcoming **Avengers: Doomsday**.

This quiz is designed for hardcore Marvel fans who know more than just the movies.

🔥 Features
- 🧠 **10 Expert-Level Questions** — Challenging trivia based on deep Marvel lore.
- 🌌 **Multiverse-Themed** — Explore concepts like incursions, alternate realities, and Battleworld.
- 🦹 **Doctor Doom Focus** — Questions about Victor Von Doom, Latveria, and his comic-book history.
- 🎬 **Multiple Marvel Universes** — Inspired by MCU, Fox, Sony, Universal, and Marvel Comics.
- 👀 **Reveal Answers** — Check answers individually or reveal all answers at once.
